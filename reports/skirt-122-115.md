# Skirt hem crease and waist stretch (#115, #122)

Date: 2026-11-11. Branch `next/skirt-122-115` from master `67564d7`. Blender 5.3.0, one NVIDIA PC.

## Root causes

1. **The skirt panels were straight trapezoids.** Each side seam was longer than the panel's center line, and the hem met the side seam at an acute angle. The extra length at both seams had nowhere to go, so the hem dropped into points at each seam. Cotton and Linen buckled at one seam or the other.
2. **Nothing resisted bending across a seam.** The front and back are separate meshes joined by zero-length seam springs. Bend links and the dihedral constraint stop at the seam, so every side seam acted as a free hinge. Stiff fabrics stayed flat on each panel and folded sharply at the seam, which curled the hem corners.
3. **The skirt was too narrow at the hip line.** The side seam ran straight from waist to hem, so the cloth reached hip girth only near the hem. On the test avatar the skirt measured about 63.7 cm around at the hip line against a 64.5 cm hip, before any ease. Stretch peaked 15 to 19 cm below the waist, at the hips.

## What changed

- **Skirt starter.** Each panel is now a slice of a cone. Its waist and hem are concentric arcs that meet the side seams at right angles, and both side seams match the 48 cm center length. The flare is set so the girth at the measured hip line is hip plus 8 cm of ease. A hip estimate smaller than the waist is treated as the waist. The flare is capped at 0.5 rad, so a very wide hip with no measured hip line falls back to the old hip-line girth and the panels stay on their side of the body. Edge numbering and sewing stay the same.
- **Seam bridges.** Compile adds bend links that cross each sewn seam. They join cloth points on either side of the seam, never two points on the seam line itself, using the same straightness rule as the existing links with the back panel unfolded into the front panel's frame. Bridges along the seam line made Shrink gather about 15% too much, so they are left out. They close with the seam over the sewing time. Their stiffness is at least that of the lightest stiff-fabric link, because a stitched seam is stiffer than the cloth around it.
- **Keep refine.** Keep's 40 refine frames run 4 solver iterations instead of 2. Live handling still runs 2.

Meshing code is unchanged. The skirt meshing baseline changed only because the starter's shape changed. Tee, Tank, Ellipse and Legacy fingerprints are identical.

## Measurements

Clean full-body test avatar, 1.1 m tall, subdivided to 66,816 triangles. Skirt starter from the avatar's measurements, 90 frames, then Keep's refine. Same harness, before and after.

| Fabric | p99 stretch at Keep | Seam crease p90 | Hem-corner crease | Hem bilateral mean / max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cotton | 1.127 → 1.073 | 77 to 79° → 29 to 30° | 39 to 40° → 5 to 12° | 0.63 / 2.93 → 0.57 / 1.82 mm |
| Linen | 1.094 → 1.057 | 80° → 28 to 29° | 73 to 78° → 25 to 28° | 0.34 / 0.94 → 0.57 / 1.78 mm |
| Denim | 1.088 → 1.021 | 79 to 80° → 18 to 19° | 86 to 88° → 13° | 0.86 / 1.96 → 0.65 / 1.28 mm |

- **Stretch** is the p99 of edge length over rest length for every cloth edge, links and seams excluded. The waist band alone (top 6 cm) ends at 1.016 to 1.045. The hip-side band is still the most stretched, at p99 1.135 for Cotton.
- **Seam crease** is the angle between surface normals on both sides of each sewn pair. The hem-corner value is the lowest pair, per side.
- **Hem bilateral** matches each hem point on the left with its mirror on the right and compares heights. On this avatar the old skirt was already under 3 mm, so this metric did not reproduce a one-sided crumple. The seam crease and the dog-ear points at the seams did reproduce, and the renders show them.
- **Hem height range** grew from about 17 to 20 mm to 37 to 39 mm. The new skirt has more flare at the hem, and its soft folds rise and fall. The pointed seams are gone. Cotton still folds softly inward near each seam.
