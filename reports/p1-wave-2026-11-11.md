## #39 Per-fabric damping

Verified on the XPBD engine. Nothing set the solver's damping input, so every fabric ran at its default of 1 per second. SPEC still described the old solver's calibration.

The stock damping is a per-point linear drag. A falling panel reaches about 9 m/s at 1.0, which is g divided by 1.0. The fix needs only `presets.py`, `xpbd.py` and `xpbd_nodes.py`. No protected file is involved.

Measured free drape of a 70 cm panel on a sphere over 240 frames (mean late motion in mm per frame):

| Fabric | 1.0 (master) | Areal-mass rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Silk | slid off by frame 230 | 2.0: stays on, 1.56 |
| Knit | 0.82 | 0.82: 5.33 |
| Linen | 0.44 | 0.74: 2.38 |
| Denim | 0.39 | 0.35: 1.06 |
| Leather | 0.40 | 0.23: 0.55 |

Damping below 1.0 leaves cloth creeping instead of settling, so the shipped rule is max(1, 0.14 / areal density). Silk gets 2.0 and every other fabric keeps 1.0.

| 1.51 m | Tee | 51 | 52 | Same, 0 open seams |

Cotton and denim garments are identical to master. Damping changes how cloth moves, not where it settles, so it does not answer "Denim and Leather look alike".
