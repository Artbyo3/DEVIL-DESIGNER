# Place Panels Around the Avatar

Arrangement places 2D pattern pieces around the character in 3D before draping. You can also simulate flat pieces without arranging them.

## Live 3D Panels

Closing a pattern shape creates a matching 3D panel. Changes to the shape update its panel.

## Choose the Avatar

1. Right-click empty canvas and choose **Garment & View > Body**, or select the character in the 3D Viewport and click **Use selected body**.
2. Press <kbd>H</kbd> to show or hide the avatar guide and placement points.

## Wrap a Panel Around the Body

1. Reveal the 3D Viewport.
2. Select a piece in the canvas or 3D view.
3. Click a blue placement point, such as **Chest Front**, **Chest Back**, **Waist**, or **Upper Arm**.
4. Repeat for the other pieces.

If the body placement point you need is unavailable, choose another visible point or use **Arrange flat** and move the piece into place.

### Fine-Tune Placement

Use Blender's <kbd>G</kbd> to move a panel or <kbd>R</kbd> to rotate it. Right-click a placement point for options such as **Arrange flat** or **Reset to 2D layout**.

## Pin Pieces in Place

Pins and tacks hold fabric during simulation.

### Static Pins (<kbd>K</kbd>)

Press <kbd>K</kbd> and click a point on a piece to hold it in place.

### Avatar Tacks (<kbd>T</kbd>)

Press <kbd>T</kbd>, click a point on the fabric, then click a point on the avatar to attach the fabric there.

### Remove Pins & Tacks

Right-click a pin or tack and choose **Remove pin / tack**, or select it and press <kbd>Delete</kbd>.
