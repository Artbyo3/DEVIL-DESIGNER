# Sewing Edges

Use the Sew tool to connect pattern edges before you simulate the garment.

## Sewing Modes

### Segment Sewing (<kbd>J</kbd>)

1. Press <kbd>J</kbd>.
2. Click the first edge.
3. Click the matching edge.

### Free Sewing (<kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>J</kbd>)

Use Free Sewing when only part of an edge should be joined:

1. Click the start and end of the first edge range.
2. Click the start and end of the matching range.

### Sew Several Edges Together

Use this when one long edge needs to join several shorter edges. Choose **Pick chain A**, click the edges on the first side in order, and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Then click the matching edges on the other side in order and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. On the seam card, start and end percentages show which portion of each side is included: 0% is the beginning and 100% is the end.

## Direct 3D Viewport Sewing

1. Reveal the 3D Viewport.
2. Press <kbd>J</kbd> or <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>J</kbd>.
3. Click the visible edges on the placed pieces.

## Direction, Reversal, & Removal

- Follow the arrows to check which way the two edges are joined.
- To reverse a seam, hover over it and press <kbd>B</kbd>, or right-click it and choose **Reverse seam**.
- To remove a seam, select it and press <kbd>X</kbd> or <kbd>Delete</kbd>, or choose **Remove seam** from its right-click menu.
- Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> to undo a removal.

## Pre-Simulation Preview & Mismatch Warning

Before draping, check the seam badges for edge lengths and the difference between them. **Pass** means the lengths are close; **Gather warning** means they differ by more than 1%. A mismatch can be intentional, and it does not stop simulation.

## Seam Shrink (Elastic Cuffs & Waistbands)

1. Right-click a seam badge.
2. Choose **Shrink: 0%, 10%, 20%, or 30%**.
3. Save the design to keep the choice.

### What Shrink Changes

When Shrink is above 0%, tighter collision helps create gathers but can change nearby folds.
